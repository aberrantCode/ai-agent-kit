Three-phase implementation roadmap

Master-skills reorganization rolls out across 26 iterations in 3 phases. Phase 1 (P0) establishes foundations with core tooling. At iteration 10, a soft gate reviews sub-skill firing evidence and proceeds without stopping. Phase 2 (P1) scales manager suites. Phase 3 (P2) completes the rollout with utilities, extensions, and closeout.
